This was a safe simulation from the corpsec-ops phishing program. No real harm was done — but in a real attack, you might have given an attacker access to your account.

Red flags in the email you received

Sender domain mismatch: The "Okta" email came from a domain that doesn't match the real Okta domain (corpsec-ops.okta.com vs. attacker-domain.tld).
Urgency language: "Your password expires in 24 hours" creates artificial time pressure to bypass critical thinking.
Generic greeting: Real Okta password reset emails address you by your registered name, not "{{.FirstName}}".
Suspicious URL: Hover over the "Renew Password" link before clicking — the destination domain should be your Okta org URL.

What to do next time

  1. Hover over links to inspect the URL before clicking
  2. Sign in to Okta directly via your bookmark, never via an email link
  3. Report suspicious emails using the Phish Alert button in Gmail/Outlook
  4. If you submitted credentials, immediately reset them and tell IT
